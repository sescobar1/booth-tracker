Import & backup

Your data is saved on this device. Back it up so you never lose it.

Your booths

Saving reloads the page. Changing a booth number leaves past items under the old number.

Back up your data

Back up once a day. A Back up to OneDrive reminder shows on your To do list each day. Tap Back up, then pick OneDrive in the share menu (on iPhone you can also choose Save to Files → OneDrive). Restore it on any phone or computer to pick up where you left off.

Automatic copies

Import from your store

Upload the sales export (CSV or Excel) and new sales go into the right month and booth automatically. Sales already in the tracker are skipped. Upload the inventory export to refresh your store inventory.
Or paste rows copied from your sales report

Reminders

Reminders show on the Dashboard to-do list. Booth rent is taken out of your your store payouts, so there is no rent reminder.
More import options

Import your profit worksheet

Choose your Excel workbook. The tracker reads each month tab and adds its purchase and sold-item lines to the matching month. Your manually added entries stay in place.

Paste or read a sales report PDF

Upload a sales report PDF, CSV, or text report. Or paste sales rows below. The importer uses the item name and the last dollar amount on each row as the payout.
