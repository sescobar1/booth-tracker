Booth Tracker

A simple app for your booth: sales, purchases, rent, mileage, receipts, and taxes, all from your phone.

Start here

Use it right away (no account needed)

Open Booth Tracker
  1. Open the link above on your phone.
  2. A welcome box asks for your first name and your booths: the booth number, what you sell there, and the monthly rent. Tap + Add another booth for each one.
  3. Tap Start tracking. That's it.
Everything you enter is saved only on your phone. Nobody else, including the person who shared this, can see it.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

It opens full screen like an app and works without signal at the market.

Everyday use

  1. Sold something or bought something? Tap the round + button. Pick the booth, type the item and price, save.
  2. Got a receipt? On Bought, tap 📷 Snap a receipt. The photo is saved with the purchase.
  3. Does your store give you a sales report? Under More → Import & backup, upload the sales export (CSV or Excel) and it fills in your sold items for you.
  4. Driving for the business? On Miles, add the places you go (and the days you go there). Regular trips log themselves; tap + Log trip for the others. It keeps your mileage log for taxes.
  5. Taxes: More → Taxes shows your business profit, the mileage deduction, and quarterly due dates.

Back up every day

Because your data lives only on your phone, back it up so you never lose it. Each day your To do list shows Back up. Tap it, then choose OneDrive, Google Drive, or Save to Files.

New phone? Open the app and go to More → Import & backup → Restore from a backup.

Optional

Get your own copy on GitHub

You don't need this to use the app. Do this if you want your own web address that doesn't depend on anyone else's site. It's free and takes about 15 minutes. A computer makes it easier.

Download the Booth Tracker kit (.zip)
  1. Make a free GitHub account at github.com/signup. Pick a username you like; it becomes part of your web address.
  2. Unzip the kit you downloaded above. (On a computer, double-click it. On iPhone, open it in the Files app and tap it.) You'll get a folder with files like index.html and a fonts folder.
  3. Create a repository (a folder on GitHub): tap + at the top right → New repository. Name it booth-tracker, choose Public, check Add a README file, then Create repository.
  4. Upload the files: in your new repository tap Add file → Upload files. Drag in everything from the unzipped folder, including the fonts folder. Tap Commit changes.
  5. Turn on your website: go to Settings → Pages. Under Branch, choose main and / (root), then Save.
  6. Wait a minute or two and refresh. GitHub shows your link: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/booth-tracker/
  7. Open your link on your phone and add it to your home screen.
Already started with the shared link? Before switching, tap Back up there. Then, in your own copy, go to More → Import & backup → Restore from a backup and pick that file. All your entries come with you.

Your repository only holds the app itself. Your sales and receipts still stay on your phone, not on GitHub.
