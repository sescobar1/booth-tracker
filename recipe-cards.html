No-Bake Cookies

Makes 16 cookies

You'll need

  • 1 stick butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup cocoa
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup Jiffy peanut butter
  • 2¼ cups oats
  • 16 cellophane bags

Let's make them

  1. In a saucepan, mix butter, sugar, cocoa & milk.
  2. Bring to a boil, stirring until it starts to boil.
  3. Let it boil for 2 minutes.
  4. Turn off the burner. Stir in vanilla & peanut butter.
  5. Gradually add the oats.
  6. Drop onto wax paper and let harden.
  7. Wrap each cookie in a cellophane bag.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes 16 palm-size cookies

You'll need

  • 2 sticks butter, cubed
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp corn starch
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 12 oz bag chocolate chips
  • 16 cellophane bags

Let's make them

  1. Cream together butter, white sugar & brown sugar.
  2. Add vanilla & 2 eggs. Mix.
  3. In a different bowl, whisk together flour, corn starch, baking soda & salt.
  4. Add wet to dry.
  5. Add chocolate chips. Save some for the end!
  6. Shape palm-size cookies and bake.
  7. Press saved chips on top right out of the oven.
  8. Cool, then wrap each in a cellophane bag.
